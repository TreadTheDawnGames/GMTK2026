## Story Rules

- The opening at depth 0 is not an encounter.
- The pre-thief story targets a roughly 15-minute first playthrough.
- Depth, not elapsed time, triggers encounters. The times below are pacing targets.
- Encounters happen in mineable tunnels entered by breaking through from above.
- The player keeps digging while the credits play.
- The Thief is a distant post-credits objective, not part of the 15-minute run.
- Upgrade-driven speed changes may move later depths, but their order cannot change.

## Approved Dialogue and Action

Spelling, capitalization, punctuation, repeated words, and the unattributed
opening voice below are intentional source text.

### Opening — Surface, 0 depth

*A bus arrives at a roadside stop. The Miner disembarks. The bus departs,
leaving the Miner at the excavation site.*

**You’re back?**

**You already know the Thief lies below.**

**Only fools find him.**

**Only fools.**

### Encounter 1 — Cheese Girl, 300 depth

**CHEESE GIRL: Mister Man? Are you ok?**

**Miner: …**

**CHEESE GIRL: Wiw you pwease come to my cheese Cafe?**

**CHEESE GIRL: Buissiness is booming!  And I’m sure you wiw find it delightfuw to visit.**

**CHEESE GIRL: …**

**CHEESE GIRL: See you soon bye!**

*Cheese girl runs away*

### Encounter 2 — Lantern Keeper, 1,400 depth

*The Lantern Keeper approaches the Miner slowly in the underground tunnel.*

**LANTERN KEEPER: Another light descending into the dark...**

**MINER: ...**

**LANTERN KEEPER: How many people do you have to meet until you start to see how lonely they are?**

### Encounter 3 — Rotini, 2,500 depth

**ROTINI: Whoa! Sorry to break it to you, but this path is in use.**

**MINER: ...**

**ROTINI: I'm Rotini.**

**ROTINI: Come on, boys! We'll find that Cafe eventually!**

### Encounter 4 — Treasure Hunter, 4,000 depth

*The Treasure Hunter mines in from the side.*

**TREASURE HUNTER: Hey! Do you always barge in on people?**

**MINER: ...**

**TREASURE HUNTER: My man, take this. It'll help.  But that treasure is MINE!**

*The Miner receives the first improved pickaxe.*

**Don’t take my act of kindness as me yielding the treasure, I’ve been hunting this for years! I just can’t bear to watch someone try to mine with such a disgraceful piece of equipment.**

### Encounter 5 — Lantern Keeper’s Warning, 5,600 depth

**LANTERN KEEPER: You kept digging.**

**MINER: ...**

**LANTERN KEEPER: Everyone thinks the Thief is waiting below. They never notice what goes missing on the way.**

### Encounter 6 — The Treasure, 7,400 depth

*The Treasure Hunter has finally reached the treasure he spent years seeking.
The Miner hears him celebrating through the rock before he breaks into the
chamber.*

**TREASURE HUNTER: Yippee! Wahoo!**

**TREASURE HUNTER: I found it. More treasure than I could ever carry.**

**TREASURE HUNTER: Everyone told me I was wasting my time, but it’s not like any of them were even trying.**

**It’s finally mine! I spent so long wondering what it would look like.**

**MINER: ...**

**TREASURE HUNTER: It's mostly just heavy.**

**TREASURE HUNTER: Take this. I don't need to dig anymore.**

*The Miner receives a second improved pickaxe.*

### Encounter 7 — Quibble, 9,200 depth

**QUIBBLE: I'M QUIB-QUIB-QUIB-QUIB-QUIBBLE!**

**QUIBBLE: HAVE YOU SEEN SEEN SEEN THE CAFE? CAFE? COFFEE? COFFEE CAFE?**

**MINER: ...**

*Quibble rapidly finishes another cup.*

**QUIBBLE: THIS IS IS IS TRAVEL COFFEE. T-T-TAKE IT.**

**QUIBBLE: IT MAKES EV-EV-EV-EVERYTHING GO F-F-FASTER!**

*The Miner receives Quibble’s permanent coffee speed boost.*

### Encounter 8 — Rotini and the Colony, 11,200 depth

**ROTINI: You again? Did you find the cheese cafe? We seem to have gotten lost in our own tunnels.k**

**MINER: ...**

**ROTINI: IT’S STRAIGHT DOWN!? Why didn’t you say so earlier?**

**ROTINI:** ***Come on boys! Dig!***

*Rotini’s rat colony begins mining downward alongside the Miner.*

### Encounter 9 — The Cheese Cafe, 14,000 depth

*The Miner reaches the cafe. Cheese Girl, the Treasure Hunter, Rotini and his
rats, Quibble, and the Lantern Keeper have gathered there.*

**ROTINI: Easy, lads. We're here.**

**CHEESE GIRL: You found it! I knew you would.**

**TREASURE HUNTER: No treasure here. Good. I was tired of carrying things.**

**MINER: …**

**Treasure Hunter: What do you mean how did I get here? You didn’t take the elevator?**

**QUIBBLE: C-C-CAFE COFFEE! WE MADE IT! DID WE MAKE IT FAST? I THINK WE MADE IT FAST!**

**LANTERN KEEPER: Stay a while. Not every minute needs somewhere to go.**

**MINER: ...**

## Implementation Contract

The target depths are the initial authored values, not permission to add
time-based fallbacks. Playtests may tune depths to keep an ordinary run near
15 minutes, but must preserve the sequence. Any terrain refactor must retain a
way to reserve the next encounter floor, open its tunnel, and capture the player
when their mined depth crosses the tunnel ceiling. The comparison must tolerate
one large hit skipping past the ceiling or floor; no exact impact target is
required.

The approved dialogue above is authoritative. Do not paraphrase its unusual
spelling, capitalization, punctuation, or intentionally unattributed opening
voice without narrative approval. Placeholder language for the Thief must not
be invented into canon without narrative approval.
